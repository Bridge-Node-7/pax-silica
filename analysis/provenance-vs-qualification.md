# Provenance Is Not Qualification

**State:** BN7 Analysis
**Verified context:** 2026-08-15

Provenance can establish origin, custody, processing history, or identity.

Qualification answers a different question: whether a material, component, process, or supplier satisfies defined requirements for a bounded use.

The distinction matters because a trusted source may still be technically unsuitable, uncharacterized, unrepeatable, unapproved, or unready for insertion into a specific system.

## Reality shadow

If the distinction is operationally useful, programs that separate provenance from qualification should identify evidence gaps earlier and avoid treating an available alternate source as a usable alternate before application evidence exists.
