# Public Boundary

This repository is **public-source intelligence only**.

Do not commit:

- private supplier or customer intelligence
- unpublished government discussions
- protected infrastructure details
- operational vulnerabilities
- nonpublic prices, capacities, customer records, agreements, or negotiations
- trade secrets or patent-sensitive methods
- controlled, export-controlled, CUI, classified, or security-sensitive information
- credentials, tokens, private keys, or sensitive configuration
- personal working notes, personal history, or internal correspondence
- private roadmaps, internal operating plans, prompts, orchestration details, or nonpublic research
- private Bridge Node 7 material not deliberately approved for public release

Public artifacts should contain only information necessary for the public product, its evidence, reproducibility, validation, and appropriate limitations.

Public inclusion does not imply endorsement, funding, affiliation, qualification, certification, validation, or mission readiness.
