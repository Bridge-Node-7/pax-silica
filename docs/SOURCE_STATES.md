# Source and Claim States

## Official
Primary government or authoritative institutional publication.

## Secondary
Independent reporting, research, or synthesis used for context or corroboration.

## Reported / Draft
Credible reporting about information not established here as final public policy.

## BN7 Analysis
Original Bridge Node 7 interpretation or analytical framework.

## Unknown
Information not adequately established.

## Superseded
A historical record replaced by a newer controlling state.

Unknowns remain visible. Secondary or reported material must not silently promote itself to official.
